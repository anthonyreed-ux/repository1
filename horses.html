Equine Nutrition

Why Is Lucerne Hay Important?

High quality lucerne hay provides a natural and well balanced diet for your horse — essential nutrition from nature.

Rich in Protein

High protein content relative to grains makes it valuable for growing animals and the formation of muscle tissue.

High Energy

Provides complete daily energy requirements for a mature horse under light work conditions from a single natural source.

Strong Limbs

High levels of calcium, phosphorus and sulphur-rich amino acids result in the development of strong, healthy limbs.

Essential Fibre

Horses need at least 30% roughage by weight in their diet. Lucerne hay provides the long fibre necessary for proper digestion.

Vitamins & Minerals

A rich natural source of Vitamins A, E, K, B, D along with essential minerals including calcium, potassium and iron.

Breeding Programs

An ideal food for breeding programs, providing the balanced nutrition needed for growing and lactating horses.

About Lucerne

What Is Lucerne?

Lucerne (Medicago Sativa), also known as Alfalfa, is a green leafy crop similar to clover. It is very high in nutrients and well noted for its exceptional qualities.

High Fibre Content

Essential long fibre for healthy equine digestion and gut function.

High Protein Content

Supports muscle development and tissue growth in performance animals.

High Mineral & Vitamin Content

Rich in calcium, potassium, iron, and Vitamins A, E, K, B, D.

High Digestibility & Energy

Excellent digestibility ensures maximum nutrient uptake and sustained energy.

Natural & Balanced

The only natural food that provides high energy, good protein and a balance of vitamins and minerals — everything needed for an elite equine athlete.

Quality Guide

Hay Quality — How to Tell the Difference

Making quality hay depends on producing lucerne of the correct moisture content at baling time. Fodder King bales hay in the optimum band of moisture conditions.

Poor Quality Hay

  • Low leaf content — less nutritional value
  • High levels of foreign matter
  • Large variations in moisture content
  • High leaf loss during handling
  • Poor shelf life in storage
  • Dustiness — can cause catarrh and hay fever
  • Musty smell — indicates mould
Too dry: Stalky, dusty, low leaf content = low food value.
Too wet: Heats in storage, becomes mouldy — can induce colic.

Fodder King Quality

  • High leaf-to-stem ratio retained
  • Minimal foreign matter & impurities
  • Optimum moisture band at baling
  • Consistent dry matter content
  • Extended shelf life when stored properly
  • Clean, dust-free product
  • Fresh, natural aroma
Key factor: A heavy bale is not necessarily quality — weight varies with moisture. Dry matter content is the key quality indicator.

Guarding Your Animal's Health

Using advanced farming techniques, our lucerne growers reduce the use of farm chemicals to a minimum. In the growing phase, only NSW Department of Agriculture approved sprays are used. In harvesting, only non-chemical moisture conditioning is employed — avoiding chemical conditioners and mould retardants that may produce allergic reactions in animals.

Fodder King Grading System

Lucerne hay sold by description using objective protein and energy test results alongside subjective criteria — so you can specify exactly which grade you need.

Choice
>19–26% Protein
>9.5–11.5 MJ Energy/kg
>65–78% Digestibility

Dry, no weather damage. Free from mould. Mainly green inside. High leaf-to-stem ratio. Fine stemmed. Free of grass & weed.

Prime
>16–19% Protein
>8.5–9.5 MJ Energy/kg
>60–65% Digestibility

Dry, minimal weather damage. Mainly green, some bleaching. Minimal leaf loss. Coarser stem acceptable. Less than 5% grass.

Medium
>12–16% Protein
>7.5–8.5 MJ Energy/kg
>54–60% Digestibility

Dry, some weather damage. Green to light brown inside. Some bleached striping. Some leaf loss. Less than 10% grass.

Budget
>9–12% Protein
>7.0–7.5 MJ Energy/kg
>50–54% Digestibility

Some weather damage. Mottled green and light brown inside. Moderate leaf loss. Thick stem acceptable. Less than 20% grass.

Grading based on nutritional test results per Australian Fodder Industry Association guidelines. Appearance is not a reliable guide to nutritional value.

Storage Hints

Store under cover, preferably in a shaded, well-ventilated shed away from damp surfaces. Rain and sunlight destroy important naturally occurring minerals and vitamins — especially Vitamin A. Handle like any other naturally produced food to maintain the high quality of your Fodder King hay.
